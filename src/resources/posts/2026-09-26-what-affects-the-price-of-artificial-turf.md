Fully installed artificial turf typically ranges from **R200 to R850 per m²**, covering excavation, sub-base preparation, edging, turf and installation. That is a guide, not a quote — but it helps to know what moves a project up or down within that range.

## 1. Turf range

Turf differs in pile height, density and how it is built for use, and each PrimeTurf range has its own installed price band:

| Range | Products | Installed, per m² | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value & decorative | Deco 20mm, Zurich 25mm | R200 – R450 | Side passages, patios, courtyard borders and budget residential lawns |
| Standard | Augusta 25mm, Spring 30mm | R350 – R600 | Everyday gardens, play areas, estate secondary lawns and balconies |
| Premium | Autumn 30mm, Summer Fun 35mm | R500 – R850 | Front lawns, estate landscaping and pool surrounds |
| Sport | Multisport 10mm | R285 – R625 | Putting greens, multi-courts and play surfaces |

## 2. Site preparation

What is underneath matters as much as what you see. Preparation can include:

- lifting an existing lawn or old artificial turf
- levelling slopes or uneven ground
- dealing with tree roots
- building a free-draining base so water runs through rather than pooling
- capping or removing irrigation

Sites with easy access and a sound, level base need less preparation than sites where material has to be moved by hand.

## 3. Size and shape

Larger, simpler areas are generally more efficient to install per square metre than small areas with many edges, curves and joins.
