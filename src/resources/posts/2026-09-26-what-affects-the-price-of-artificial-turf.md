Fully installed artificial turf typically ranges from **R450 to R850 per m²**. That is a guide, not a quote — but it helps to know what moves a project up or down within that range.

## 1. Turf grade

Turf differs in pile height, density and how it is built for use. A dense, soft lawn for a family garden, a hard-wearing surface for a busy commercial space and a short, fast putting surface are different products, and they are priced differently.

## 2. Site preparation

What is underneath matters as much as what you see. Preparation can include:

- lifting an existing lawn or old artificial turf
- levelling slopes or uneven ground
- dealing with tree roots
- building a free-draining base so water runs through rather than pooling
- capping or removing irrigation

Sites with easy access and a sound, level base need less preparation than sites where material has to be moved by hand.

## 3. Size and shape

Larger, simpler areas are generally more efficient to install per square metre than small areas with many edges, curves and joins.
